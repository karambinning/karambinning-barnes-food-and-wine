The shop

A neighbourhood corner shop, kept properly.

Barnes sits on Castle Street between the town centre and the rise toward Crendon Lane — the kind of small independent shop a road needs but rarely keeps. Wine, beer and spirits behind the counter; bread, milk, eggs and tins to the back; a paper on the rack and a chocolate bar on the way out.

Open six days a week. Nothing fancy — just the things you actually want at the time you want them, from someone who knows the road you live on.
